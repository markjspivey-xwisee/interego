INTEREGO HEALTH CONNECTIONS

Terms of use

Personal source connections, operated by the owner of this Interego deployment. Updated October 31, 2026.

Authorized personal use

Use this bridge only for your own records or records you have the patient's permission and the source provider's authorization to access. Caregiver use requires the appropriate patient or proxy approval. An Interego sign-in alone does not grant access to another person's health record.

Connection permissions

Each source requires its own permission. The medical-record connection requests read-only access. Sync is user-triggered; this version does not continuously poll. Provider availability, permissions and the source's coverage determine which records can be imported. Oura imports and agent access remain disabled pending provider authorization for this use.

Private storage and authorized agents

Before enabling imports, review the data-handling disclosure. Records and source refresh tokens are stored with server-managed encryption; the deployment operator controls the encryption key. The operator and hosting infrastructure providers support the service. Authorized Interego agents acting for the same account can read full imported records under general authorization, without a separate prompt or notification for each read. This version does not provide a user-facing access-history report.

Sharing and stopping imports

Connecting a source does not publish its records. A linked Atlas publisher can separately review and publish a sanitized clinical snapshot, and opt into updates of the same supported fields after later Duke syncs. Review the public destination, included fields and exclusions before publishing. Automatic public updates can be paused separately from imports; an already published copy may have been saved by readers. Other sharing requires separate user direction.

You can pause imports, disconnect a source, and revoke access through the source provider. Existing imported records are retained indefinitely until the deployment operator removes them, including after disconnection or account closure. This version has no self-service record-deletion screen; removal requires deployment-operator action.

Record limitations

This is a personal record integration, not a provider's electronic health record or a clinical decision system. Imported information may be incomplete or out of date. Missing information does not establish a negative finding. Medication orders do not establish that a dose was administered. Check source records and dates, and ask the treating care team about diagnosis and treatment decisions.
