Create an account, or sign in

There is no password and no sign-up form. A passkey — Windows Hello, Touch ID, or whatever unlocks your machine — both creates the account and signs you in, and the first time you do it a storage pod is provisioned for you. Everything you write lands there and stays yours.

The identity you pick decides which pod your words land on, permanently. A passkey gives a u-pk-… pod; a wallet key gives a u-eth-… one. They are different accounts, not two doors to one account — signing in the other way later shows an empty workspace, because it is a different pod.

The passkey ceremony happens in your own browser rather than in this window. That is not a shortcut: the proof has to be made at the identity server's own origin, and a page loaded from disk cannot produce one. Your browser also means a real platform authenticator rather than a key this program made up.

Create a workspace

Five records on your own pod, published in order: a shape, a role table, the workspace record naming both, a grant to yourself, and your acceptance of it. Nothing is created anywhere else, and nobody else is touched.

Open a workspace by its IRI

Every workspace is a URL. Paste one and this client dereferences it, resolves its convener, and looks on that convener's pod for a grant naming you.

Your agent's model

Your agent runs on a model subscription you have already signed into on this machine, under your own account. This app never sees that credential — it starts the tool you already installed and reads its answer. Nothing is billed to anybody else, and there is no shared key.

Link your Discord account

Run /workspace link in Discord. The bot replies privately with two values — its agent id and your own Discord user id. Put them here and this app publishes the delegation on your pod for you, so you never have to run a tool call by hand.
Members
