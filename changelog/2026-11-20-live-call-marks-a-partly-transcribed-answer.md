## 2026-11-20 — Live call: an answer of which only part was transcribed is marked

The marks added earlier the same day (#986) covered an answer the transcriber
failed on entirely. Where it failed on part of what the learner said in an
exchange and wrote the rest, the exchange was saved as an ordinary answer with no
mark: the import and a judge had no sign that some speech was lost. A reviewer in
another session found it within the hour, with a reproduction in both orders.

Such an exchange now carries the words that were written and a mark,
`pev:partlyTranscribed`. The import puts `partly-transcribed` on the turn. The
words are the learner's and may be quoted; a judge is told not to hold against
the learner anything such a turn does not say, and a reviewer sees the mark
beside the turn. An answer cut to the record's length limit is marked the same
way. The judge's prompt gained a sentence, so its template hash changed again.

A scenario that names none of the mark terms still gets the earlier, unmarked
form, and no call saved before these changes is made reliable by them.
