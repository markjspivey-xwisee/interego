## 2026-11-18 — Role-play player: a spoken reply is heard whole, and matched more strictly

Found by running the player by voice. Two faults, one behind the other.

The recogniser stopped at the first pause, about half a second, so a reply said
in two breaths was heard as its first words: "to recap", "I can see both". The
player now keeps it listening through pauses and ends the reply itself, after two
seconds with nothing new heard, or when the learner taps the microphone again.
Only words the recogniser settled on are matched, and only when all of them
settled: a settled start with an unsettled continuation is not matched as the
start alone. If it ends on unsettled words,
or on an error, no reply is offered; before, the confirmation could appear after
a network error. A recogniser the learner has replaced with a later one is
ignored: its late end no longer switches off the one that is listening. Tapping
a written reply lets go of the recogniser before aborting it, so words it still
had queued are not offered against the next step.

The matching took any overlap of words: "to recap" was the whole recap, and "I
cannot refund the duplicate today" was the reply that refunds it. A spoken reply
now has to share at least two of a reply's words and a quarter of them, is not
matched to a reply when it carries a "not" that the reply does not, and is not
matched at all when two replies fit it equally. A match is still only offered:
the learner accepts it or taps a reply.

Not settled by this: it compares words, not meaning. A paraphrase in other words
is not matched and the learner taps a reply; the first third of a reply still
matches. A reviewer reads what was said either way.
