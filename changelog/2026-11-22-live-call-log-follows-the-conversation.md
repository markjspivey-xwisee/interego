## 2026-11-22 — Live call: the on-screen log follows the conversation

The log of a live call grew down the page. From about the third exchange the
newest lines were below the bottom of the screen, and a recording of a
two-minute call showed its opening lines for the last fifty seconds while the
conversation went on out of view. A reviewer in another session reported the
same from the recording.

The log is now a panel of its own that scrolls and keeps to the newest line, so
what is being said, the state of the call and the button that ends it are on
screen together. A learner who has scrolled back to read an earlier line is
left there until they return to the end. The panel is marked as a log for
screen readers.
