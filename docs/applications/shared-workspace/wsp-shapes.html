Shared workspace shapes 1.0 wsp:

Namespace https://markjspivey-xwisee.github.io/interego/applications/shared-workspace/wsp-shapes#

SHACL shapes for the shared workspace. These run at PUBLISH, before anything reaches a pod: a record that violates one is refused with a 422 and never lands, so a malformed membership cannot exist even briefly.

Machine-readable form: wsp-shapes.ttl (Turtle) — the authoritative source. This page is a human-readable projection of it.

What this is for

wspsh:WorkspaceShape
Exactly one convener and one role profile, both dereferenceable.
wspsh:MembershipGrantShape / wspsh:MembershipAcceptanceShape
Every principal must be an https: or did: URL. A membership naming an identifier nobody can dereference asserts something about a party who cannot be looked up — and a urn: here would break cross-organisation membership outright, since the other organisation has no way to resolve it.
wspsh:EntryShape
Requires wsp:seq: without it a stream cannot be replayed in order and a gap cannot be told from a deletion.

Checked in CI against pySHACL as well as our own engine, so a shape cannot mean one thing to us and another to a conformant reader.
