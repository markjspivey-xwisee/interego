pev: — Practice Evidence (vertical application) pev:

Namespace https://markjspivey-xwisee.github.io/interego/applications/practice-evidence/pev# · vertical application, non-normative.

Vocabulary for conversation practice with evidence a reviewer can stand behind: scenarios with a rubric, practice attempts and their transcripts, judgments whose quotes the platform verifies against the learner's own words, platform-computed results, reviews by a qualified person, reopenings, redaction, the role registry and the audit rows of statements built for a learning record store. Payload terms carried inside Interego (iep:) Context Descriptors: the descriptor layer supplies identity, provenance, temporal validity, modal status and trust; pev: supplies the practice domain. It extends no L1, L2 or L3 ontology. Calibration terms describe agreement with the reviewer, never accuracy.

Machine-readable form: pev.ttl (Turtle), the authoritative source; the SHACL shapes are at pev/shapes. This page is a human-readable projection of the Turtle; each term below is identified by a hash IRI under this base (e.g. pev:Attempt = https://markjspivey-xwisee.github.io/interego/applications/practice-evidence/pev#Attempt).

pev:ScoringRule Individual of pev:PlatformRuleScoring rule
For a pass gate the platform computes the points as the sum, over criteria, of the weight times the value of the chosen level, and the maximum as the sum of each weight times the value of that criterion's top level. The percent is points over maximum, rounded half up to one decimal. The pass rule is met when points times 100 is at least the minimum percent times the maximum and every required level is reached. An unverified criterion counts at its lowest level until a reviewer sets it. For feedback only none of this is computed: the result is the levels alone.
