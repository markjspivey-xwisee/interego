Work Redesign — vertical vocabulary wrd:

Namespace https://markjspivey-xwisee.github.io/interego/applications/work-redesign/wrd# · 132 terms.

Vertical application of Interego — not part of the protocol, not a reference implementation of the protocol. One specific use case among many possible; the Interego protocol does not depend on any of this. Vocabulary for deciding which work moves to agents: how knowable each task is, who does it (people and registered agents as one workforce), honest valuation ranges, decisions that stay Hypothetical until an accountable person countersigns and the world answers, and grants agents earn and lose. Payload terms carried inside Interego (iep:) typed context descriptors.

The regimes, methods, allocation levels, classification rules and default grant policy below are published policy: the work-redesign engine reads them from this file at runtime, and every record cites the sha256 of the exact text it was checked against.

Machine-readable form: wrd.ttl (Turtle) — the authoritative source; SHACL shapes at wrd/shapes.ttl. This page is a human-readable projection of it; each term below is identified by a hash IRI under this base (e.g. wrd:Decision = https://markjspivey-xwisee.github.io/interego/applications/work-redesign/wrd#Decision).

Classes

wrd:Acknowledgement Class
A person's own signed acknowledgement of, or objection to, the role a plan names them for on a task.
wrd:AgentRun Class
One run of a registered agent on a task, with the agent's own assessment and, once a person reviews it, that person's verdict. Only reviewed runs count toward calibration or a grant.
wrd:AllocationLevel Class
Who does a task: a person, a person with an agent's help, an agent drafting for a person who decides, an agent acting with a person auditing, or an agent acting within an earned grant.
wrd:Classification Class
How knowable a task's cause and effect is (its regime), placed by a published rule from evidence answers a named person signed.
wrd:ClassificationRule Class
One row of the published decision table that places a task in a regime from evidence answers. Rows are tried in order; the first match wins.
wrd:Decision Class
A submitted plan, its countersign by an accountable approver, and later its measured outcome, as versions on one graph. Countersigned means approved; the prediction it carries stays unmeasured until an outcome is recorded.
wrd:Grant Class
An earned permission for one registered software agent, on its declared model, to act alone on one registered kind of task. Revoked by a superseding Retracted version; checked on every use.
wrd:GrantPolicy Class
The numbers an agent's person-reviewed runs must reach before it may act alone on a kind of task.
wrd:HolderCopy Class
A receipt that a person or agent took a copy of every record that names them or carries their signature, to a pod they hold or as a download.
wrd:Method Class
The way of changing work that a regime calls for.
wrd:Plan Class
A proposed change to who does which tasks, bound to the classifications and task texts it was checked against. Either valued (a priced change) or a probe plan (safe-to-fail probes on Emergent work, never priced).
wrd:Prediction Class
The band a decision predicts; its status says whether the world has answered yet — a countersigned decision is Asserted, its prediction stays unmeasured until an outcome.
wrd:Regime Class
How knowable the relation between cause and effect is in a piece of work. Read first: the regime picks the method and limits which changes are allowed.
wrd:RegimeSource Class
Where a regime placement came from.
wrd:RoleRegistry Class
The admin-signed list of the keys that may act on a pod: their roles (approver, operator, worker, agent), the person reference an admin attests is behind each human key, each agent's accountable principal and declared model, the registered kinds of task, and the pod policy.
wrd:Task Class
One piece of work in a workflow: its description, the person or agent who does it today, every party named on it, and the quotes it cites. Asserted only when every named party ratified the current text.
wrd:Valuation Class
The seeded Monte Carlo valuation of a valued plan: ranges in, P10/P50/P90 out, for the cash figure and for the figure if freed hours are redeployed, with every input's basis, the cost breakdown, sensitivity and pilot size.
wrd:ValuationMethod Class
A versioned statement of how a valuation is computed, so a stored valuation can be recomputed from its inputs and seed.
wrd:Workflow Class
A unit of work a team does repeatedly, captured by hand or from a document, listing its current tasks. Hypothetical: a description of how work is done.

Published policy individuals

wrd:defaultGrantPolicy Individual · wrd:GrantPolicy
An agent earns a grant on one kind of task from at least 20 person-reviewed runs on its declared model, a reviewed hit rate of at least 0.9, at least 5 person confirmations from at least 5 distinct registered reviewers the admin attests are different people, and at least 0.9 precision on the runs it called ready. Critical work additionally needs ceil(3 / 0.01) = 300 clean runs (rule of three, 95% one-sided). Re-classification is suggested once at least 5 reviewed runs show more than 0.2 reworked.
wrd:Emergent Individual · wrd:Regime
Cause and effect are visible only in hindsight. Work changes only through safe-to-fail probes with amplify and dampen conditions, and is never given a point valuation.
wrd:Evident Individual · wrd:Regime
Cause and effect are obvious and stable: the same steps give the same result, the method is documented, the output can be checked and feedback is fast. The only regime in which an agent may act within a grant.
wrd:ExpertAnalysis Individual · wrd:Method
Have someone who knows the work analyse it before changing it, and value the change only on their evidence.
wrd:Knowable Individual · wrd:Regime
Cause and effect can be worked out by analysis or expertise. No agent acts within a grant here, and a valued change needs expert evidence.
wrd:LevelAgentActsPersonAudits Individual · wrd:AllocationLevel
A registered agent acts; a named person audits its output. Only for output that can be checked.
wrd:LevelAgentActsWithinGrant Individual · wrd:AllocationLevel
A registered software agent acts alone, only while it holds an earned, unrevoked grant for this kind of task on its declared model. Never available to a person.
wrd:LevelAgentDraftsPersonDecides Individual · wrd:AllocationLevel
A registered agent drafts the output; a named person decides on it before it takes effect.
wrd:LevelPerson Individual · wrd:AllocationLevel
A person does the task.
wrd:LevelPersonWithAgentAssist Individual · wrd:AllocationLevel
A person does the task and may use an agent's help; the person decides and is the performer.
wrd:ProbePortfolio Individual · wrd:Method
Run a few small safe-to-fail probes, each with a hypothesis, a condition to amplify it and a condition to dampen it, inside a timebox; read what happens before deciding anything larger.
wrd:RuleOnSignedAnswers Individual · wrd:RegimeSource
A published rule (wrd:ClassificationRule) applied to answers a named person signed. It is not trajectory evidence and must not be read as agp's 'derived' source; it confers no calibration authority on the regime.
wrd:RuleR0 Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
No other rule matched: the answers do not place this task in a regime. Answer the unknowns, or run a probe to find out. No regime is ever assumed by default.
wrd:RuleR1 Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
The situation is unstable (situationStable = unstable).
wrd:RuleR2 Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
The outcome depends on others (outcomeDependsOnOthers = yes), or the method cannot be known in advance (methodKnown = not-knowable), or the outcome partly depends on others and the same steps rarely give the same result.
wrd:RuleR3a Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
The same steps always give the same result, the method is documented, the outcome does not depend on others and the situation is stable, BUT the output cannot be checked (or checkability is unknown) or feedback takes weeks or longer (or is unknown): read as Knowable, because Evident needs a checkable output and fast feedback.
wrd:RuleR3b Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
The same steps always give the same result, the method is documented, the outcome does not depend on others, the situation is stable, the output can be checked before or after it takes effect, and feedback arrives within minutes or days.
wrd:RuleR4 Individual · wrd:ClassificationRule
The method is documented or can be worked out by expert analysis, the outcome depends on others not at all or only partly, and the stability of the situation is known.
wrd:StabiliseFirst Individual · wrd:Method
Act to restore order before redesigning anything; classify again once the situation is stable.
wrd:Standardise Individual · wrd:Method
Write the steps down, make the output checkable, and move the work to whoever (person or agent) performs it reliably, measured.
wrd:Turbulent Individual · wrd:Regime
No discernible cause and effect; the situation is unstable. Act to stabilise first; no allocation change is allowed.
wrd:valuationMethodV1 Individual · wrd:ValuationMethod
Independent triangular draws (low, likely, high) taken in a fixed order: units per week current then proposed, value per unit, hourly cost, one-off cost, then per task runs per unit, the task's hourly cost if it overrides the workflow's, the seven current-state inputs, and for changed tasks the seven proposed-state inputs. The generator is xoshiro128** seeded from the first 128 bits of sha256(canonicalJson({planIri, inputs})); percentiles are type 7 (linear interpolation); 5000 draws unless the record says otherwise. Correlation between inputs is not modelled. The change ramps in linearly over the stated ramp months. Two figures are reported: the cash figure, in which freed person-hours count only where the work is left unfilled, and the figure if freed hours are redeployed at current hourly cost. NPV discounts monthly at (1 + annual rate)^(1/12) - 1 over the horizon, net of the one-off cost; payback is the first month the discounted cumulative turns non-negative.
