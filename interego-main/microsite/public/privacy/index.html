Hosted Interego Privacy Notice

Effective November 6, 2026

Foxxi Mediums Inc, based in North Carolina, United States, operates hosted Interego. This notice covers the core hosted service, including its accounts, guest access, pods, dashboard, and connections to apps and tools. Contact mark.spivey@xwisee.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information we handle

  • Accounts and sign-in. We handle your account identifier, profile information you provide, and details used to verify sign-in. Depending on the method, these can include passkey credential identifiers and public keys, wallet addresses, decentralized identifiers, and sign-in and session information.
  • Guest access. We issue temporary account and pod identifiers. Supported Play experiences use a key held by your browser, and we process its public key or identifier and the guest access deadline.
  • Terms acceptance. When you agree to the hosted service terms during signup, we record the accepted version and acceptance time in your private account record.
  • Pod records and descriptions. We handle records you submit, store, or share, along with identifiers, dates, signatures, source information, and sharing settings. Records can include information about you or others.
  • Connected apps. We handle connection details, permissions you grant or revoke, and the requests and results needed to operate those connections.
  • Technical information and communications. Hosted requests can generate IP addresses, request times, browser or client information, and service, error, and security logs. If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you provide.

We receive information from you, your browser or client, the apps you connect, and people or organizations that share records with you. The dashboard uses browser session storage for sign-in. Supported Play experiences may store a sign-in key in your browser.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate hosted pods, carry out authorized requests, manage permissions, and provide support. We also use information as needed to operate and secure the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, send service communications, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Where EEA or UK data protection law applies, our legal bases include performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and compliance with legal obligations. If we rely on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing.

Who can receive information

  • Railway. Railway hosts the core service and pod database in California, United States.
  • Pinata and IPFS. When certain records are published, we send separate record descriptions to Pinata for storage on IPFS. Descriptions can include identifiers, dates, signatures, and other record information. This can happen even when the record contents are encrypted. Information made available through public IPFS can be retrieved and copied.
  • People, pods, and apps you authorize. These recipients can receive information available under the permissions you grant. A connected AI service can receive readable information when an authorized request returns it. Recipients can keep copies and handle them under their own terms and privacy practices.
  • Public access. People can retrieve and copy publicly published records. Some account identifiers and descriptions can be discoverable through identity or discovery features. If you publish wallet payment instructions, they can include an amount, currency, and wallet address.
  • Legal and protective disclosures. We may disclose information where legally required or where reasonably necessary and lawful to investigate abuse, protect people or systems, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Our main site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to retrieve those files, which sends basic request information such as your IP address. The core hosted pages do not change their behavior in response to a browser’s Do Not Track setting. Connected services you use can apply their own privacy settings and practices.

Encryption and sharing

Interego supports encryption for protected record contents. A record’s description is handled separately and can contain readable information. Hosted components can process readable contents to carry out authorized requests, depending on how the record is published and accessed. Removing a permission stops future access through that grant. Recipients can retain copies already received. Review permissions and record descriptions before publishing or connecting a tool.

Retention and removal

We keep account information and hosted records as needed to provide the service and carry out authorized requests. Our current Railway plan gives us a 30-day window to view service logs through Railway. We keep other operational, support, or security records as needed for their purpose and applicable legal requirements.

Closing a regular account disables sign-in and leaves a limited closure record. The pod and its stored records remain in hosted storage. To request removal of the records themselves, use available record controls or email us. Removal from active access can leave copies in retained versions, recovery copies, or records held for legal or security purposes. Recipients and public networks can keep copies outside hosted Interego.

Supported Play guest access is currently configured to expire after 24 hours unless you enroll a regular account before expiry. The separate Try Interego evaluation account is generally scheduled for cleanup about seven days after creation unless you claim it. Guest expiry and record cleanup can occur at different times. Some guest records and a limited closure record can persist after expiry.

Where information is processed

Foxxi Mediums Inc is based in the United States, and Railway hosts the core application and pod database in California. Connected services, publication providers, and recipients may process information in other locations. Contact us for information about the destinations and safeguards applicable to your information.

Your choices and rights

You can review available sharing and connection settings. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a decision about a request. Email mark.spivey@xwisee.com to make a request. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify it. Where applicable, you can also complain to your data protection authority. If an organization controls the records, you can contact it about those records.

Children and notice updates

Hosted Interego is intended for adults aged 18 and older. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action. We will publish updates here with a new effective date and provide additional notice of material changes where required.
